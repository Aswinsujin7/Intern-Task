Frequently Asked Questions

Instagram DM automation is a programmatic messaging system that automatically listens for incoming direct messages, Story mentions, or Reel comments and responds instantaneously using predefined conversational funnels. It enables businesses to qualify leads, answer frequently asked questions, and route inquiries 24/7 without requiring manual human monitoring for every inbound notification.

When a user interacts with your profile (such as sending a DM or commenting a keyword on your Reel), Meta's servers emit a real-time webhook payload. Our secure automation infrastructure processes this event, checks configured trigger conditions, and calls the official Meta Graph API to dispatch the appropriate response bubble, interactive button, or media asset back to the user within milliseconds.

Yes. Under Meta's platform policies, automated direct messaging is exclusively available for Instagram Business or Instagram Creator accounts. Additionally, your Instagram account must be connected to a Facebook Page within Meta Business Suite, and messaging permissions must be granted to the automation application. Personal accounts cannot use API-level messaging automation.

Yes, through Meta's officially supported Comment-to-DM trigger. When a follower comments on your Feed Post or Reel containing your designated keyword (e.g., "PRICE", "GUIDE", "WORKSHOP"), our system can automatically send a private direct message containing your link or lead magnet. Please note that Meta messaging policies enforce a 24-hour standard messaging window following user-initiated interaction.

Absolutely. Our workflows prompt users step-by-step to supply their email address, business name, phone number, and budget range. The inputs are validated in real time (e.g. checking for correct email formatting) and immediately transmitted via webhooks to your CRM (such as HubSpot or Salesforce), email marketing platform, or Google Sheets.

Yes, our system incorporates an automatic Human Handoff Protocol. When a prospect selects "Talk to Support" or when a query is flagged as complex, the bot pauses automated replies for that user thread and dispatches a notification to your team via Slack or email. When your team replies manually via Meta Business Inbox or the Instagram app, the automation remains paused to ensure a natural human interaction.

Yes, 100%. Dolo Technology develops automations exclusively on the official Meta Messenger API for Instagram (Graph API).
